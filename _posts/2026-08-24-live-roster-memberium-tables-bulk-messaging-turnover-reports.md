title: "Built Live Roster Tooling for Memberium Tables, Bulk Messaging, and Turnover Reports"
short: "A membership org can now **manage roster tables, send bulk messages, and run turnover reports** from custom WordPress tooling instead of scattered exports."
---

A membership organization running on **Memberium** needed operational visibility beyond what out-of-the-box roster screens provide. Program staff wanted roster-style tables, a practical way to message groups in bulk, and reporting when membership turnover spiked.

I developed the **Live Roster** plugin with views tuned to their Memberium data model—roster tables staff actually use day to day, bulk messaging workflows, and turnover reports that summarize movement over time ([ptf-live-roster commit history](https://github.com/PurpleTurtleCreative/ptf-live-roster/commits)).

The tooling keeps day-to-day membership operations inside WordPress rather than ad hoc spreadsheets and one-off SQL.
