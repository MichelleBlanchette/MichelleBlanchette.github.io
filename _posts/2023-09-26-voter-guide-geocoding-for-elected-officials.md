---
title: "Auto-Filled Elected Officials on Voter Guide Forms with Geocod.io"
short: "Voters enter an address once, and Formidable Forms **fills in districts and representative contact fields** via Geocod.io—no manual lookup."
---

A Platyform client needed a voter guide where constituents could look up who represents them without wading through separate government sites. The form had to capture an address and immediately surface the right elected officials and contact paths.

I integrated **Formidable Forms** with **Geocod.io** so each submission geocodes the address and populates federal and state district fields, school district, and senator email data in the same flow. Editors keep control of the form layout; the plugin handles the lookup logic behind the scenes.

The result is a civic-tech workflow that reduces data-entry errors and helps voters reach the right office faster.
