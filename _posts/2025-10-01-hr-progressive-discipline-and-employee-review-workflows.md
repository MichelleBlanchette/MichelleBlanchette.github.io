title: "Streamlined HR Progressive Discipline and Employee Review Workflows"
short: "I connected **Formidable Forms and Elementor** pieces into coherent HR workflows—discipline steps and review cycles—without exposing sensitive details on the public site."
---

A multi-location operator needed HR processes—progressive discipline tracking and periodic employee reviews—to live in the same WordPress stack their sites already use, with clear steps for managers and HR without custom paper trails.

I implemented workflow support through **Formidable Forms** configurations and **Elementor**-backed snippets in **ptf-snippets** and **ptf-umbrella-enhancements**, focusing on routing, visibility, and hand-offs between steps rather than one-off PDFs ([3948f6d6](https://github.com/PurpleTurtleCreative/ptf-snippets/commit/3948f6d6), [55d83f82](https://github.com/PurpleTurtleCreative/ptf-umbrella-enhancements/commit/55d83f82)). Nothing sensitive is published on the marketing site; access stays inside authenticated admin and member contexts.

Managers get a repeatable process; HR gets audit-friendly structure without a separate SaaS subscription for every location.
