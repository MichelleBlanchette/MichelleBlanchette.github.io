As Top Cruise Deals was forming, the founder reached out to me to build the business’s flagship website which would be the entire customer-facing business. It is the storefront, the deal catalog, and the primary path to revenue, so it had to be production-ready from day one. It needed to be fast, editor-friendly, and able to publish limited-time offers by cruise type, cruise line, and destination, with reliable scheduling and expiry, user-friendly performance, and technical SEO to drive conversions.

As the sole founding engineer, I owned the project from concept through architecture, implementation, and launch, advising on product decisions, content workflows, and technical tradeoffs along the way. I built an optimized custom WordPress FSE block theme with ACF-powered schemas for Deals, Cruise Lines, Cruise Ships, and Cruise Destinations, including the relationships that power listings, detail templates, and nested deal URLs under each cruise line. Custom blocks such as a filterable Deals List, Cruise Lines List, Destinations List, breadcrumbs, deal terms, ship grids, and more compose the templates that editors use to rapidly publish on-brand landing pages. Editors can schedule deals ahead of their start date, and the site automatically manages the content lifecycle to effortlessly ensure accurate, time-sensitive advertising. Additionally, I exposed custom Google Merchant and Google Ads data feeds to streamline marketing operations using the same data already entered by the editorial team. I also designed for edge-friendly full-page caching on the managed host, lean frontend assets, crawlable URL hierarchy, custom sitemaps, breadcrumb markup, optimized SEO metadata and schema, and robots rules that keep admin and API surfaces out of the crawl budget.

The site launched as a responsive, accessible, editor-owned platform with a single deployable theme bundle. The expertly crafted data model and single-source-of-truth architecture that I designed keep content management efficient which has empowered the business to keep its team and operations incredibly lean with surprisingly low operating costs. In its first year online, this site has brought in over $1 million in cruise line sales.
