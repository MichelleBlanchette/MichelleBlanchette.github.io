title: "Built a Custom Full Site Editing Theme to Launch TopCruiseDeals.com"
short: "From Figma to launch, I built TopCruiseDeals.com as a **custom block theme** so the team can publish and schedule cruise deals without touching code. 🚢"
---

TopCruiseDeals needed a fast, editor-friendly website where the team could publish limited-time cruise deals and browse them by cruise line, ship, and destination. Instead of reaching for a page builder, I built a custom WordPress block theme with ACF-powered data schemas for Deals, Cruise Lines, Cruise Ships, and Cruise Destinations.

I created custom blocks like a filterable Deals List with manual selection and a three-level sort, along with cruise line, ship, and destination detail templates powered by block bindings so editors only manage content once. Deals can be scheduled ahead of their start date and are automatically trashed after they expire, so the site keeps itself current.

The theme launched with responsive, accessible templates and a single deployable bundle for the client's managed host, and the team has been publishing deals through the block editor ever since.
